Privacy Policy

Effective 9 August 2026 · Applies to the FloodedArea website (floodedarea.com) and the FloodedArea Android app (com.floodedarea.app), which is a wrapper around the same site.

The short version. FloodedArea is a free flood-watch map for the Philippines. To be useful it needs your location, and anything you report is published on a public map. You can browse the whole map without an account. We do not sell your data and we do not run advertising.

Flood reports are public. When you submit a report, its location, water depth, the display name you entered, and any photo you attach become visible to everyone using FloodedArea — including people who are not signed in. Please do not report from inside your home if you do not want that spot shown, and do not include faces, licence plates, house numbers, or documents in photos.

1. Who we are

FloodedArea is an independent, non-commercial community project covering Metro Manila, Rizal, and surrounding areas. Contact: floodedareaph@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Location

With your permission, the app reads your device's precise location to centre the map, work out how far you are from nearby floods, attach coordinates to a report you submit, and locate you for an SOS rescue request. While a trip or live view is active the app may follow your location continuously until you close it. You can refuse or revoke this permission in your browser or Android settings; the map still works, but distance, "report here", and SOS will not.

Account information

If you create an account or sign in with Google, we store your email address, display name, and profile picture URL, plus an internal user id and role. Signing in is only required to report, confirm a report, or send an SOS — browsing is open to all.

What you submit

  • Flood reports — display name, district, precise coordinates, severity, water depth, trend, road condition, and the time.
  • Photos — optional. Compressed and stored by us. Photos can reveal more than you intend; see the notice above.
  • SOS rescue requests — precise coordinates, district, city, barangay, and an optional free-text note. Shared with responders and administrators so they can reach you.
  • Feedback and confirmations — anything you send us directly, and taps confirming someone else's report.

A device identifier for guests

If you report without an account, we generate a random identifier and keep it on your device (baha_guest_id_v1). It is not linked to your name or email. Its only purpose is anti-abuse — limiting how many active reports one device can post. Clearing your browser storage resets it.

Stored on your device, not sent to us

We use your browser's local storage for your session token and profile, plus preferences: language, notification and live-view toggles, report mode, sidebar layout, trip state, and a per-session visit flag. These stay on your device. Clearing site data removes them and signs you out.

Usage measurement

We count total visits to show a public visitor tally, and we use Statcounter for basic traffic statistics. This involves your IP address and general browser information. We do not build advertising profiles.

3. How we use it

  • To show the live flood map, evacuation centres, road conditions, and alerts.
  • To compute distances, safety status, and routes relative to where you are.
  • To publish the reports you choose to submit.
  • To pass SOS requests to responders and administrators.
  • To prevent spam and abuse.
  • To keep the service working and understand overall usage.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. Community chat

The chat bubble embeds a public room hosted by tlk.io, a third-party service. Messages go to tlk.io, not to us. We do not store them, and our deletion controls do not reach them. The room is public and anonymous — treat anything you type as visible to strangers, and do not post personal details there.

6. How long we keep things

  • Flood reports expire automatically — they are time-limited by design, since stale flood data is misleading.
  • SOS requests are kept while they are being handled and for a reasonable period afterwards as an operational record.
  • Account details are kept until you ask us to delete the account.
  • On-device storage stays until you clear your browser data or uninstall.

7. Your choices

  • Location — refuse or revoke the permission at any time in your browser or Android settings.
  • Browse anonymously — no account is needed to view the map.
  • Access or delete your data — email floodedareaph@gmail.com and we will delete your account and associated personal data. Note that a published report may already have been seen, shared, or cached elsewhere.
  • On-device data — clear site data in your browser, or clear the app's storage in Android settings.
  • Notifications — turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

8. Children

FloodedArea is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

Traffic is encrypted in transit over HTTPS, and access to administrative functions is restricted. No service can promise perfect security, and we would rather say that plainly than imply otherwise.

10. This page

This page loads no fonts, no analytics, and no external resources of any kind. Opening it tells us nothing beyond an ordinary web-server request.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the effective date above and, where the change is significant, note it in the app.

12. Contact

Questions, corrections, or deletion requests: floodedareaph@gmail.com.
