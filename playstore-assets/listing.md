## Full description — max 4000

```
FloodedArea shows you where it is flooding right now in Metro Manila and Rizal — how deep the
water is, whether it is still rising, and which roads have stopped being passable. Reports come
from people who are standing there, so the map reflects the street, not a forecast.

WHAT YOU GET

• A live flood map. Every report carries a depth in centimetres, a trend (rising, steady or
  receding) and a road status, colour-coded so you can read the situation at a glance.
• A straight answer about your own location. FloodedArea tells you whether you are inside a
  flood-affected area, how far the nearest flooding is, and — when water is rising — a rough
  estimate of how long it might take to reach you.
• Your own monitoring area. Set a watch radius from 1 to 20 km and keep an eye on the places
  that matter to you.
• Evacuation centres, with capacity, how full they currently are, and a contact number.
• Emergency hotlines, collected in one place instead of a screenshot in your gallery.
• Rainfall warnings and advisories, shown across the top of the app while they are in effect.
• Trip monitoring. Tag the stops along a route and get told when new flooding appears near any
  of them.
• An SOS request, if you are trapped and need rescue.

REPORTS THAT STAY HONEST

Flood water moves, so old reports are worse than no reports. Every sighting expires on its own —
sooner for ankle-deep water, later for chest-deep — and anyone nearby can confirm a report to
keep it alive and mark it as still true. What you see on the map is what people are seeing now.

REPORTING TAKES A FEW TAPS

Drop a pin, pick a severity, drag the depth. That is the whole thing. You can report as a guest
without making an account; your reports are simply marked unverified until other people confirm
them.

BUILT FOR HERE

Metro Manila and Rizal, with the Marikina River basin and the low-lying barangays that flood
first. Available in English and Filipino.

Free, and free of ads.

FloodedArea is a community tool, not an official government source. It does not replace PAGASA
bulletins or the instructions of your LGU and local disaster office. In an emergency, always
follow the authorities — and never wade or drive through fast-moving water.
```

## Notes for the rest of the listing

- **Category:** Weather (Maps & Navigation is the alternative; Weather matches how people search
  for this during a typhoon).
- **Tags/keywords:** flood, baha, Marikina, Metro Manila, Rizal, evacuation, rescue, PAGASA,
  typhoon, road conditions.
- **Privacy policy URL:** `https://floodedarea.com/privacy.html` — must be entered exactly.
- **Data safety form:** must agree with that policy: precise location (including continuous
  tracking), account email/name/picture, report payload with photo, SOS coordinates and note,
  a guest device UUID, and the third parties listed in the policy.
- **Content rating:** answer honestly about user-generated content. The app carries user text,
  user photos and — when the admin toggle is on — an embedded public chat room, which raises the
  rating band. See the UGC item in `../PLAYSTORE_TODO.md`; it is unresolved and can fail review
  on its own.
- **Disclaimer:** the closing paragraph of the full description is deliberate. A crowd-sourced
  safety app that reads as authoritative invites both a rejection and real-world harm.
